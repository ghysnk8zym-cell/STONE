## A. Project purpose and approved scope

**Business.** STONE is a luxury natural-stone (marble, limestone, slate, sandstone, quartzite, concrete) retailer/fabricator website. Concept, palette, typefaces (Lusitana headings, Inter body; PT Serif only as the Cyrillic fallback of Lusitana) and imagery are already approved in Figma and must be preserved exactly. "Premium" means craft, not more gold, blur or motion.

**Users and journey.** Guests browse the catalog and product pages and use a cost calculator; Clients keep an account (orders, favourites, profile); an Admin manages products and orders. Intended journey: home → catalog → product → calculator → (cart/checkout, **not built**) .
